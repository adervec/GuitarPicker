# Overlaps (house standard 27)

Where GuitarPicker and another app, or the hub, hold the same data, do the same job, or could feed
each other. A register for the maker to choose from: **an entry is not permission to build it.**
Format and states as in `../CoworkSyncHub/OVERLAPS.md`.

Reviewed: 2026-11-22

- [idea] GuitarPicker + classical-journey: playable public-domain songs on one side, "what to listen for" on the other -> link a piece to its playable arrangement and back
- [idea] GuitarPicker + MusicStudio: synthesized backing bands and generated stems are the same need -> a MusicStudio stem as a GuitarPicker backing track, read from a `shared-stems.json` MusicStudio owns
- [idea] GuitarPicker + SpeechImprover + LanguageLab: GuitarPicker's karaoke grades a singer's pitch from the mic, SpeechImprover and LanguageLab capture and score speech -> one shared mic/pitch-detection piece in font.js instead of three engines
- [idea] GuitarPicker + ChessRetabled + HandwritingHelper + hub: GuitarPicker keeps its own daily practice streak while the hub draws streaks from the session ledger -> decide whether the app streak stays or reads the hub's
- [idea] GuitarPicker + hub: GuitarPicker's coin and cosmetic economy rewards practice inside one app, the hub's character sheet rewards it across apps -> decide whether coins stay app-only
